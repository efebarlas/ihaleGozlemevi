## Task
Propose 2–3 distinct visual directions, each grounded in this product rather than generic app conventions. The look should carry credibility: a careful public record, like a newspaper data desk or an annotated official document, not a startup dashboard.

Each direction is a set of design tokens, delivered as a CSS file of custom properties: `design/unguided/tokens/<short-name>.css`. It covers:
- colors for light and dark mode, where dark is designed, not inverted;
- the type scale and font stack (system fonts or self-hostable OFL fonts only; no external font CDNs);
- spacing;
- the flag/annotation treatment;
- card or list treatment;
- the source-link treatment.

Apply each token set to the **real** Bugün, Gün, İhale detayı and Yöntem pages, with real tenders, and look at them at 375 px in light and dark. No static mocks.
- You may edit `style.css` locally to try them, but leave the repo's `style.css` as you found it.
- The builder will apply your recommendation and add a hidden switch between the sets.
- Save phone-width screenshots of each direction (Bugün and one İhale detayı, light and dark) in `design/unguided/screenshots/directions/`.

## Rules that are not taste (SPEC §9.2, §6.1, §6.2)
- **No alarm red, warning icons or siren colors** on flags. Flags look like marginal annotations on a document.
- **No colors tied to Turkish political parties.** Avoid AKP orange/yellow-blue, CHP red-white, MHP red, İYİ light blue, DEM purple-green-yellow, and similar.
- **The source link is always visually present.**
- **Readability and neutral language win ties:** WCAG AA contrast, 44 pt touch targets, scannable numbers (tabular figures for money), Turkish characters rendered correctly.
- **Avoid "default look" tells:** generic rounded cards with soft shadows everywhere, indigo/purple gradients, emoji as icons, pill chips for everything, uniform spacing with no hierarchy, dark mode as plain inversion.
